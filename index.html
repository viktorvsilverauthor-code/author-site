Viktor V. Silver writes about people who get knocked down and stay down — and then get up. The writing is lived, not learned from a book. By training he is a lawyer. By trade he is a storyteller. Neuropathy took his hands. It took keyboards, took typing, took the easy road from page one to The End. It left him his voice, so he dictates — every word, out loud, the slow way. He does not believe in shortcuts. Not in prose. Not in people.

His books are only beginning to be seen.

Essays on Stoicism and the long work of growing up. A science-fiction trilogy that stretches across centuries. A children's book that asks the first question: what makes a person who they are? One book, asked three ways — and the man who dictates it is nowhere near done. There is much in him that has not been said yet.
